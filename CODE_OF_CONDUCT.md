# Code of conduct

We want this repository to be a constructive place to build, learn, and share
voice-agent integrations.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and generous when giving feedback.
- Assume good intent and focus discussion on the work.
- Share only information you are authorized to disclose.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption,
or sharing another person's private information are not welcome here.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness a violation, contact an OmniDimension maintainer
privately. Reports are handled respectfully and as confidentially as practical.

Maintainers may remove comments, close contributions, or take other action to
keep the project constructive.
